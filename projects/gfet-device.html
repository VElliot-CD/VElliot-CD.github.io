Dec 2023 — Dec 2024 · Team project · Exploratory device simulation

Exploratory graphene FET modeling for biosensing

Reviewed GFET biosensing mechanisms and built two- and three-dimensional device simulations, using bias sweeps to examine output, transfer and electric-field characteristics.

Context

An exploratory device-modeling study related to GFET biosensing, not a fabricated biosensor or a biological-sample validation study.

My contribution

  • Contributed the biosensing literature review and team research report.
  • Built 2D and 3D simulations, defined meshes, materials and electrodes, and interpreted simulated characteristics.

Methods & tools

  • Silvaco DeckBuild with TonyPlot / TonyPlot3D visualization.
  • Swept gate and drain bias to examine output curves, transfer curves and electric-field distributions.

Evidence

  • Completed exploratory models and analysis documented in the team report.
  • Discussed model applicability and thermal effects; no claim of chip fabrication or clinical/biological testing.
