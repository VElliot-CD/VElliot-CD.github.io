Power Conversion & Control Laboratory Portfolio

Four linked experiments covering a PWM generator, rectification and linear regulation, a boost converter, and modulation and spectral analysis of a single-phase full-bridge inverter.

Context

This lab portfolio demonstrates power-electronics fundamentals from circuit simulation to bench measurement, with waveform, ripple, spectrum, and THD data used to explain circuit behaviour.

My contribution

  • Independently completed data collection, waveform analysis, simulation comparison, and report writing across four linked experiments.
  • Analysed Boost conversion, filter capacitance, PWM modulation, and full-bridge inverter spectra quantitatively.

Methods & tools

  • Combined LTspice simulation with laboratory bench testing
  • Used oscilloscopes, digital multimeters, and waveform / spectrum analysis
  • Compared square-wave, bipolar PWM, unipolar PWM, and filter-capacitance conditions

Evidence

  • The Boost converter raised 5 V to 10.0 V and 20.0 V, with about 70 mV and 72 mV ripple.
  • Increasing capacitance from 2.2 mF to 6.9 mF reduced measured ripple from 1.40 V to 0.74 V.
  • Fourier and THD analysis was used to compare inverter modulation strategies.
